FaceTok Reels HQ backend patch

Changes in server.js:
- Replaces the old 320 kbps low / 720 kbps high fixed encodes.
- Low rendition: AVC/H.264 Main, CRF 23, up to 540x960 portrait (960x540 landscape), maxrate 1.8 Mbps, AAC 96 kbps.
- High rendition: AVC/H.264 High, CRF 18, up to 1080x1920 portrait (1920x1080 landscape), maxrate 8 Mbps, AAC 128 kbps.
- Preserves yuv420p + avc1 + faststart for broad Android compatibility.
- Keeps the original uploaded Reel source as a mezzanine/master for future re-encoding rather than deleting it after the first transcode.
- Existing already-transcoded Reels cannot recover detail that was discarded by the old 720 kbps rendition; upload a new Reel to judge the new encoder.

This implements the same public architectural principles Meta describes for Reels (master ingest + multiple quality renditions + quality-targeted encoding), but it is not Meta's proprietary production encoder/ABR system.
